### Weekly Drop-In Sessions

Ongoing weekly sessions young people can join any time. Each one stands on its own, so nobody falls behind if they miss a week or two. New participants welcomed each week.

*Accordion (See details):*

Sessions can cover songwriting, lyric writing, dynamics, permission to create, self-compassion as a musician, performance prep, recording, mentoring and free jams.

Some crowd favourites: the Worst Jam Ever, Lyric Circle, Cut-Up Lyrics, the 60-Second Jingle and the Conductor Game.

What the group wants, the group gets. If they want to write a song in an hour, that's exactly what we'll do.

### Tailored Programs

A shorter or longer program, a particular age group, or a focus you choose.

*Accordion (See details):*

Every school, council and youth organisation is different, and so are the young people in your community. If you'd like a different length, a focus beyond music like self-compassion, resilience or connection, a group with more or less experience, or your staff trained in our methods and activities, we'd love to talk.

We love a challenge. If you don't see a program that suits you, get in touch with Dave and we'll work to make it happen.

## A4. Who it's for

- **Ages:** Anywhere from 11 to 18.

## A5. How it works

1. **Planning call.** We talk through your young people, your space and what you're after.
2. **Lock it in.** We agree the program, dates, numbers and who runs it, and send through our paperwork.
3. **We deliver.** We turn up and run every session.
4. **We report back.** Attendance, feedback, photos (with consent) and the recording.

## C1. Program description

Good Noise Project runs after-school music programs where young people write, play and record original music together. Every program is built on three Cs:

- **Create:** getting out of your own head and writing
- **Collab:** connecting with others and jamming as a band
- **Capture:** recording what the group made

The 8-Week Program includes:

- a planning call before the program starts
- eight weekly 2-hour sessions at your venue
- session themes and activities, planned and delivered by us
- drum kit, mics and PA [CHECK: match your gear list]
- a finale of your choice: a performance at a festival or event, an in-house concert for family and friends, or a live recording at your venue
- a recording of the group's song for every participant
- an end-of-program report within two weeks of the final session: attendance, participant feedback, photos (with consent) and the recording

## C2. 8-Week Program in full

Sessions follow three themes: **Permission**, **Play** and **Connection**. Permission is given every session.

*Graphic: permission-play-connection.png*

| Week | What happens | Theme | Discussion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm up together, first jam, pick a theme for the song, sketch some chords, start humming melodies | Permission | |
| 2 | Lyric writing methods and activities, exploring chord progressions and melodies | Permission | |
| 3 | Song structure mostly locked, lyrics nearly done | Permission into Play | |
| 4 | Adding colour to the song: hooks, counter melodies, harmonies | Play | Realignment when lost |
| 5 | More time on the extras: a solo, a bridge, dynamics, dropping in and out | Play | The details |
| 6 | Run-throughs and a nerves check-in | Connection | Self-compassion |
| 7 | The finale | Connection | Revisiting all the themes |
| 8 | Reflection and projection: how last week felt, where they want to take their music, and who will keep them accountable | Connection | Where do you want to take this? Who will keep you accountable? |

**Finale options:**

- **Performance at a festival or event.** A 15–20 minute set at a festival, market or community event you already run. We lock the slot in by week 2 so the group has something to aim for.
- **In-house concert.** A gig for family and friends at your venue.
- **Live recording at your venue.** If a performance isn't the right fit, we record the group playing their song in the space they've been rehearsing in.

Whichever you pick, every participant takes home a recording of the song.

## C3. Implementation considerations

- **Target population:** Young people anywhere from 11 to 18 with some playing experience: a few chords, a basic beat or being comfortable singing.
- **Staffing:** Hand it over to us, with no staff time needed in sessions and one nominated contact person. Or run it with us, with one staff member joining each 2-hour session.
- **Space and equipment:** A room that can handle a loud band, [PLACEHOLDER: three or more] power points, and somewhere nearby for small groups to split off. No venue? We'll help you find one. Participants bring their own instruments where they can. We supply the drum kit, mics and PA. [CHECK]
- **Group size:** Up to 12 participants.
- **Session length:** 2 hours, once a week, after school (time to suit, typically 4–6pm or 5–7pm).
- **Registrations:** Through your booking system, or ours by arrangement.
- **Program adaptability:** Shorter or longer formats, specific age groups, groups with more or less experience, a focus beyond music such as self-compassion, resilience or connection, and staff training in our methods and activities.
- **Reporting:** An end-of-program report within two weeks of the final session.
- **Australian context:** Developed and delivered in Western Australia.
