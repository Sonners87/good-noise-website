# PART A — `/photography-policy`

## Photography, Filming and Recording Policy

*Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Next review: 20 August 2027*

Good Noise Project takes photos and video at our workshops. This page explains exactly what we capture, what we do with it, how we look after it, and how you can change your mind at any time.

If you'd rather ask a person than read a policy, email Dave at dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au or call 0413 626 240.

### Why we do it

We use photos and video to show families and schools what actually happens in a Good Noise room — on our website, on our social media, and in presentations to schools, councils and funders when we're seeking support for future programs.

That's the whole list. We use this material for promoting Good Noise Project and nothing else.

### What we capture

During a workshop we may take:

- Photos of participants playing, writing and working together
- Photos where nobody is identifiable — hands on instruments, a lyric sheet, a wide shot of the room, people from behind
- Short video of the group working, and of the song being performed at the end
- Audio recordings of the song the group writes

Some of this is also for the participants themselves. Every group receives a recording of the song they write, whether or not their family has agreed to us using any of it.

### Consent: how it works

**Nothing is used without permission, and permission is never all-or-nothing.**

When you book, you choose separately whether we may use:

- images where your child isn't identifiable
- identifiable images online
- identifiable images in print and in presentations
- audio including your child's voice or playing
- your child's first name alongside an image or quote

You can tick all of these, some of them, or none. There's also a plain "No thanks" option that declines everything.

**Consent is never a condition of taking part.** Your child's place is exactly the same whatever you choose, and we won't ask why.

**Your child gets their own say.** We ask the young person directly, in their own words, on the same form. If a parent agrees and the young person doesn't, **the young person's answer is the one we follow.** Australian privacy guidance treats most young people aged 15 and over as able to make this decision for themselves, and we think it's the right thing to do regardless of age.

**Consent expires.** Every permission lapses automatically three years after the workshop. We don't ask anyone to agree to something permanent. If we want to keep using material beyond that, we ask again.

**On the day.** Before we start, we tell participants there'll be a camera around and that anyone can step out of a shot at any moment — no reason needed, no questions asked. Whoever is taking photos works from a list of who has agreed to what.

### Who takes the photos

Photos and video are taken by Dave Sonntag, or occasionally by someone he's engaged for the day. Anyone taking photos on our behalf:

- is briefed on this policy before the workshop starts
- works from the consent list for that group
- holds a current Working With Children Check if they'll be present with participants
- hands over all material at the end and keeps no copies

### How we store it

Photos, video and audio from our workshops are:

- transferred as soon as possible to a single dedicated device, into a password-protected folder used only for Good Noise Project material
- kept separate from personal devices, personal photo libraries and personal cloud backups
- deleted from cameras, phones and memory cards once transferred
- accessible only to Dave Sonntag

Files are named and filed by workshop so we can find and remove an individual's material quickly if we're asked to.

We keep material only while consent for it is current. At the three-year mark it's either deleted or we seek fresh permission.

### What we never do

- Sell images, video or audio, or licence them to anyone else
- Use them to advertise another organisation or product
- Publish a participant's surname, school, suburb or contact details
- Publish location data — geotagging is off and we don't identify where a participant lives or studies
- Publish anything featuring a participant whose family or who themselves declined
- Use anything for a purpose the person didn't agree to

### Changing your mind

**You can withdraw or change your permission at any time, for any reason, and you don't have to explain.** Email dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au or call 0413 626 240.

We'll stop using the material and remove it from anything we control — our website, our social media, our presentation decks — within seven days, and confirm to you when it's done.

One honest limitation: we can't recall material that's already been printed and distributed, or that someone else has already downloaded or reshared. We'll remove it from everything within our control and stop all future use.

Participants can ask us directly, without going through a parent.

### Photos taken by other people

Other participants and their families may take their own photos at our workshops. We ask everyone not to photograph or post images of other people's children, and we say so at the start of every program. We can't guarantee it, and it's worth being aware of.

If something involving your child is posted by another family and you'd like help with it, tell us and we'll do what we can.

### Concerns or complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled photos, video or audio of your child, contact Dave Sonntag at dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au or 0413 626 240. We'll respond within five business days and work with you until it's sorted.

### Our commitments

Good Noise Project voluntarily follows the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations. Dave Sonntag holds a current Working With Children Check (WWC 561262) and is SafeTALK trained.

# PART B — `/privacy`

## Privacy Collection Notice

*Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Next review: 20 August 2027*

Good Noise Project (ABN 15 933 602 313) is run by Dave Sonntag in Perth, Western Australia. This notice explains what personal information we collect when you book a workshop, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Questions: dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au or 0413 626 240.

### Where we stand legally

As a small business we're not bound by the Privacy Act 1988. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles anyway, because we work with young people and we think you're entitled to the same standard you'd get from a larger organisation.

### What we collect and why

**About the participant** — name, date of birth, address, preferred instrument, and how they're getting to and from the venue.
*Why:* to run the program, confirm eligibility, and know who's responsible for each young person at the start and end of each day.

**Emergency contacts** — names and phone numbers.
*Why:* so we can reach someone quickly if we need to.

**Health information** — allergies, medical conditions, medication carried, and any support needs.
*Why:* solely to keep your child safe and to make the two days work for them. This is sensitive information, we ask for your express consent to collect it, and it's seen only by Dave.

**Parent or guardian details** — name, email, phone, address.
*Why:* to communicate about the booking and to contact you during the program.

**Your consent choices** — what you and your child agreed to regarding photos, video, audio and the song recording.
*Why:* so we can honour them, and so we have a record of what was agreed.

**Payment** — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

We only collect what we need to run the program safely. We don't collect anything for advertising or profiling.

### Who else sees it

- **Stripe** processes payments. We don't hold card details.
- **Netlify** hosts our website and stores form submissions.
- **Our email provider** carries our correspondence with you.

That's it. We don't sell your information, share it with marketing companies, or pass it to anyone else — except where we're required by law to do so, or where there's a serious and immediate risk to someone's safety.

### How we look after it

Booking information is held in our website's form system, protected by a password only Dave has access to. Anything downloaded is kept on a password-protected device in a folder used only for Good Noise Project.

### How long we keep it

| Information | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Health and medical information | Deleted 30 days after the workshop ends |
| Booking and contact details | 2 years, then deleted |
| Consent records | 3 years, matching the life of the consent |
| Photos, video and audio | While consent is current — see our [Photography Policy](/photography-policy) |

If you join our mailing list, that's a separate thing you opt into, and you can unsubscribe from any email we send.

### Seeing, changing or deleting your information

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you or your child, correct anything that's wrong, or delete it. Email dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au. We'll respond within 30 days, and there's no charge.

Deleting your information may mean we can't complete a booking — we'll tell you if that's the case before we act.

### Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, contact Dave Sonntag at dave@goodnoiseproject.com.au or 0413 626 240. We'll acknowledge within five business days and aim to resolve it within 30.

### Website

Our website uses only what's needed to make it work and to understand basic traffic patterns. We don't run advertising trackers and we don't build profiles of visitors.

# PART C — Before you publish

All values are filled. Three things left, none of them writing:

1. **Make the storage description true before the workshop, not after.** The policy commits you to a single dedicated device, a password-protected folder used only for Good Noise, material kept out of personal photo libraries and personal cloud backups, and cards wiped once transferred. If you shoot on your phone and it auto-syncs to iCloud, that sentence is false the day you publish it. Set the folder up now and check your backup settings.

2. **Check the "who else sees it" list is complete.** It currently names Stripe, Netlify and your email provider. If anything else touches booking data — a mailing list tool, a calendar, a scheduling app — add it.

3. **Get it read.** A WA community legal centre or WACOSS will look over this for a small organisation cheaply or free. Having done it is itself worth saying in a school pitch.

**Diarise 20 August 2027.** The pages state a review date, so it needs to actually happen. Same day, put a recurring note in your calendar to check the three-year consent expiries — the first batch from this workshop lapses 30 September 2029.
